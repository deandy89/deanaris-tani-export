Quality Workflow

From Toraja Highlands to Global Ports

01

Selective Harvesting

Only mature, red coffee cherries handpicked from high-altitude Toraja volcanic soils.

02

Semi-Washed Hulling

Pulping and wet-hulling to preserve authentic earthy undertones and vibrant acidity.

03

Drying & Sorting

Sun-dried to maximum 12% moisture with multi-stage screen grading and defect sorting.

04

Export Packaging

Packed in 60kg jute bags with complete export documentation and prompt ocean freight dispatch.

Our Products

Toraja Green Coffee Beans

Cultivated in the fertile highlands of South Sulawesi, our Toraja Arabica and Robusta green coffee beans deliver exceptional character for specialty roasters worldwide.

Toraja Arabica

Specialty Grade (Cup Score 80+) | Semi-Washed (Wet-Hulled) | Moisture: Max 12%

Cultivated in high altitudes, offering a well-balanced body, bright acidity, and deep notes of dark chocolate and fruit.

Toraja Robusta

Special Grade Export | Semi-Washed | Moisture: Max 12%

A bold, heavy-bodied bean with a clean cup, delivering strong, earthy, and rich nutty notes perfect for espresso blends.
